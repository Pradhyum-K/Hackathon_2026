About Us

Our Mission

We strive to ensure that all people with disabilities get the necessary services and resources they need to be part of a more inclusive community.

We are a community-driven organization that focuses on connecting people with all sorts of disabilities with appropriate resources and services. We have a loyal employee-base where each individual is people-driven, empathetic, and open to connecting with individuals with a variety of disabilities.

We also have a Community Center, where members of our website can attend weekly activities to socialize and meet other individuals with similar life-experiences. Our CC is located in Bellevue, at 12345 NE St, Bel-Red, 98004. We host weekly activities at the CC that allows participants to connect with other individuals. See the schedule below!
